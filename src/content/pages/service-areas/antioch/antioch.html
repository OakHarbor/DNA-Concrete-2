FAQs About Concrete Coatings in Antioch, CA

What Are Epoxy Coatings Best Used For?

Epoxy coatings work best in spaces that see frequent traffic, heavy loads, or exposure to chemicals and liquids. In Antioch, we often install epoxy floors in warehouses, showrooms, automotive service centers, and residential garages. Epoxy creates a sealed surface that prevents concrete from breaking down under pressure, moisture, and abrasion.

Do You Apply Concrete Coatings To Outdoor Surfaces?

Yes. While epoxy is best suited for indoor use, we install polyurea and polyaspartic coatings that handle outdoor exposure. Patios, walkways, and pool decks in Antioch neighborhoods like Mira Vista Hills benefit from coatings that hold up under heat, rain, and UV rays. These finishes add slip resistance and protect the concrete from weather-related cracking.

How Long Does Epoxy Flooring Take To Install?

Most jobs take one to three days from prep to final cure, depending on the square footage and complexity. Industrial coatings with safety striping or multi-layer flake systems may take slightly longer. We'll give you a project timeline during the quote so you know what to expect.

Can You Coat My Pool Deck Or Decorative Patio?

Yes. We apply slip-resistant coatings to pool decks, especially where the concrete has worn smooth over time. If you're looking for a decorative patio, we offer stained and sealed finishes that bring out natural tones or add color variation. These finishes are sealed for weather resistance and long-term durability.

What Kind Of Maintenance Do Coated Floors Need?

Concrete coatings are low maintenance. You can clean them with a soft mop, light detergent, or a floor scrubber in commercial settings. For outdoor coatings, a pressure washer on a low setting usually works. You don't need to wax or polish them.

What Is Joint Expansion Repair And Why Might I Need It?

Concrete expands and contracts as temperatures shift. Expansion joints help control that movement. Over time, the filler breaks down. We clean out old filler and replace it with a semi-rigid joint material that protects the slab and prevents cracking. This is especially important in large warehouses and garages.

How Long Do Your Coatings Last?

With proper surface prep and regular cleaning, epoxy and polyurea coatings typically last 10 to 15 years. Some high-traffic areas may need touch-ups sooner, but most coatings hold up for a decade or more. We'll walk you through the expected lifespan for your specific application.

Is Polished Concrete An Option Instead Of Coating?

Yes. If you want a smooth, reflective surface without applying a coating, polished concrete is a great option. We mechanically grind and seal the surface for a long-lasting finish that looks modern and clean. It's a popular choice for Antioch office buildings and showrooms.

Can You Add Custom Colors Or Decorative Flakes?

Absolutely. We offer a range of color blends, flakes, quartz, and metallic finishes. Whether you want a clean, modern look or something that resembles stone or terrazzo, we can customize the finish to fit your design goals.
