FAQs Regarding Concrete Coatings and Repair in Lodi, CA

What Makes Epoxy Coating A Good Fit For Concrete Floors?

Epoxy creates a protective surface that resists wear, stains, and chemicals. It bonds tightly to concrete, forming a hard, smooth layer that holds up in high-traffic spaces like garages, retail stores, and workshops. It also gives the floor a clean, finished appearance that's easy to maintain.

What If I'm Interested In A Stained Or Polished Look Instead?

Epoxy isn't the only finish we offer. We also provide concrete staining, polishing, sealing, and joint expansion repair. These are great options for spaces that need a decorative or natural concrete finish. We'll help you decide what fits best for your property and goals.

How Long Does It Take To Coat A Concrete Floor With Epoxy?

Most epoxy floor projects take two to three days, including prep, application, and cure time. Larger garages or commercial spaces may require additional time, especially if crack repair or grinding is involved. We'll always give you a clear timeline before we begin.

Do You Offer Epoxy Floor Coatings For Residential Garages?

Yes, we install epoxy coatings in large residential garages throughout Lodi and nearby towns. Whether you're working on cars, storing equipment, or turning your garage into a home gym or showroom, epoxy gives you a polished and resilient surface that holds up.

Can You Add Custom Colors Or Textures?

Absolutely. We offer a wide range of color blends and can include flake finishes, metallics, or high-gloss topcoats. We also add texture for traction, which is helpful in both commercial and residential settings. Every coating system is customized to match the client's style and needs.

What Kind Of Prep Work Is Involved Before Applying Epoxy?

The surface must be clean, dry, and free of any coatings or sealers. We start with grinding the surface to open up the pores and remove imperfections. We also repair cracks, chips, and damaged joints to create a stable foundation for the epoxy system.

Will Epoxy Coatings Crack Or Peel Over Time?

Not when installed correctly. Our prep process helps the epoxy bond deeply with the concrete. We use professional-grade products that flex slightly with temperature changes and resist peeling. With normal use and proper care, your epoxy floor can last over a decade.

What Types Of Commercial Properties Use Epoxy Floors?

We work with auto shops, retail stores, offices, coffee shops, medical clinics, manufacturing sites, and many others. Epoxy coatings perform well in areas that experience foot traffic, equipment movement, or chemical exposure. They're also easy to clean and maintain, which makes them ideal for client-facing businesses.

Can Epoxy Be Installed Over Old Concrete?

Yes, but the condition of the existing surface matters. If the concrete is in good shape, we can apply epoxy directly after surface preparation. If there's an old coating, stains, or damage, we'll evaluate the floor and take care of grinding, patching, or removal as needed.

Do You Offer Slip-Resistant Options?

We do. We can add anti-slip aggregates during the coating process, which helps reduce the risk of falls in wet or high-traffic areas. This is especially helpful in commercial kitchens, garages, medical offices, and retail stores where spills or moisture are common.

How Long Before I Can Walk Or Drive On The Floor?

Most epoxy coatings cure within 24 to 48 hours for light foot traffic. For vehicle traffic, we recommend waiting at least 72 hours. We'll give you a complete timeline based on the weather, size of the project, and the specific materials used.
