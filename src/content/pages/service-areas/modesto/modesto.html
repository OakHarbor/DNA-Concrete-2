FAQs About Pool Deck Repair And Resurfacing in Modesto

Can You Resurface A Painted Concrete Pool Deck?

Yes. We remove old paint during surface preparation before applying any new material. Grinding the surface exposes clean concrete and opens its pores, which allows the new coating to bond tightly. Once the surface is clean and stable, we apply an epoxy system that delivers a smooth, durable finish. This approach eliminates peeling, improves appearance, and helps the new coating last for years.

How Long Does Epoxy Pool Deck Resurfacing Take?

Most projects take between two and four days from start to finish. The exact timeline depends on the size of the deck and the amount of repair work needed. After the final topcoat is applied, the surface typically cures within 24 hours and can handle light foot traffic shortly after. We plan the schedule around your needs so you can enjoy your pool again quickly.

Do You Fix Cracks Before Resurfacing?

Yes. Repairing cracks is an important part of the resurfacing process. We clean and fill any visible cracks or surface defects using outdoor‑rated patching materials. For larger areas of damage or surface spalling, we grind the affected spots and rebuild them before coating. These steps create a smoother, stronger surface and prevent problems from returning after the new finish is installed.

Is Epoxy Slippery When Wet?

No. We include traction additives in every pool deck coating we install. These additives improve grip without making the texture uncomfortable under bare feet. The result is a surface that stays safe and stable around water while still feeling smooth enough to walk on comfortably.

Can I Choose The Color And Finish?

Yes. We offer a wide range of finishes, from classic solid colors to decorative flake blends, quartz textures, and metallic coatings. During your estimate, we'll show real sample boards so you can visualize how each option looks in natural light. Many homeowners choose tones that match tile, landscaping, or exterior colors to tie the pool area together.

How Long Will The New Surface Last?

With proper preparation and regular cleaning, epoxy pool deck coatings can last ten to fifteen years or longer. For commercial pools or high‑traffic outdoor spaces, a fresh topcoat every few years helps maintain protection and color depth. The durability comes from both the coating material and the thorough surface preparation we perform before applying it.

What If I Have An Old Coating Or Overlay?

If your existing surface is in decent shape and still bonded, we may be able to coat directly over it after grinding. If the old material is peeling or separating from the concrete, we'll remove it completely to reach a clean base. This ensures that the new epoxy system bonds correctly and lasts as long as possible. We evaluate each deck individually to recommend the best approach.

Do You Offer Decorative Designs Around The Pool?

Yes. We can incorporate decorative elements into your pool deck design, including custom color borders, stone‑like flake blends, and contrasting patterns that highlight the pool's shape. These design touches give your pool area a more upscale look without the maintenance of tile or pavers. Decorative epoxy and stained concrete can also extend to connected patios or walkways for a consistent outdoor style.

How Should I Prepare My Pool Area Before The Project Starts?

Before work begins, we ask clients to clear furniture, planters, and pool accessories from the deck area. If the pool is open, covering it helps keep dust from settling during grinding. Our team protects surrounding features and handles cleanup at the end of the project. We'll explain exactly what needs to be moved or covered during the site visit.

When Is The Best Time Of Year To Resurface A Pool Deck In Modesto?

In Sacramento's climate, spring and early fall are ideal times for resurfacing work. Temperatures are steady, and humidity is low, which helps coatings cure evenly. However, our materials are designed to perform year‑round, so we can schedule work in other seasons as well. We'll recommend the best time based on your pool's use and the local weather forecast.
