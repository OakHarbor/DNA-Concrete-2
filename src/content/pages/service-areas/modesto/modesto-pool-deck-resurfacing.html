What Kind of Pool Decks Can We Resurface?

We work on backyard pool patios, apartment complexes, hotels, country clubs, and public swim centers. Whether the deck is small or spans hundreds of square feet, the approach is similar. If the concrete is structurally sound, we can resurface it---even if it's stained, pitted, or has multiple cracks. Painted decks, old overlays, or flaking finishes can also be repaired and replaced with a longer-lasting system.

We can resurface:

  • Plain concrete decks

  • Previously painted or sealed decks

  • Pebble finishes and broom finishes (once prepped)

  • Faded coatings or overlays

If the slab is crumbling or sinking in large areas, we may recommend partial replacement first. But most decks we see in Modesto are good candidates for repair and resurfacing.

Our Resurfacing and Repair Process

We begin by walking the site, checking for damage, and identifying areas that need attention. Then we prep the surface by grinding it with heavy-duty equipment. This step removes paint, dirt, sealers, and smooths out the surface. Cracks and holes are cleaned and filled with outdoor-rated patching compounds. Once the surface is stable and clean, we apply our epoxy coating system in layers.

The system includes a base coat to help the material bond to the concrete, followed by a texture or flake layer if selected. Finally, we apply a clear topcoat to lock in the design and provide added durability. The full process takes a few days, depending on the size of the job and the finish selected. Once complete, the deck cures quickly and can often be walked on the next day.

Epoxy Materials That Hold Up in the Heat

We use professional-grade epoxy systems designed for California's climate. These coatings hold up to heat, pool chemicals, and direct sunlight without breaking down. They do not flake or soften under stress. The top layer includes traction additives that help keep the surface safe for walking, even when it's wet.

Our finishes range from clean, solid-color designs to more decorative options like quartz or flake blends. These textures add dimension and extra grit for foot traffic. For clients who want a more dramatic look, we also offer metallic coatings that shift with the light and create visual movement.

Benefits of a New Epoxy-Coated Pool Deck

A resurfaced deck makes the pool area easier to clean and more comfortable to use. The surface resists peeling, staining, and mold growth. It stays cooler than plain concrete, and it won't become slippery from pooled water. The smooth, sealed coating also keeps dirt and debris from working into small cracks. You'll spend less time maintaining the surface and more time enjoying the space.

FAQs About Pool Deck Repair and Resurfacing

Can you resurface a painted concrete pool deck?

Yes. We remove old paint during surface prep. Once the concrete is ground down and cleaned, the epoxy system can bond properly and form a long-lasting finish.

How long does epoxy pool deck resurfacing take?

Most resurfacing projects take two to four days. The timeline depends on the size of the deck and the number of repairs needed. After the final coat is applied, the surface usually cures within 24 hours.

Do you fix cracks before resurfacing?

Yes. We address cracks, small holes, and other surface defects before any coating is applied. These repairs help the coating last longer and create a smoother final appearance.

Is epoxy slippery when wet?

We add texture to every system we install. These additives reduce slipping without making the surface rough or sharp. The result is a safer deck that still feels good under bare feet.

Can I choose the color and finish?

Yes. We offer a wide range of colors, textures, and styles. From subtle natural tones to bold metallic finishes, we help you pick a look that works well with your space and your taste.

How long will the new surface last?

With proper prep and good maintenance, epoxy systems can last ten years or more. In high-traffic settings, a clear recoat every few years helps preserve the look and feel of the original installation.

What if I have an old coating or overlay?

If your existing surface is still stable, we may be able to coat over it. If it's peeling or loose, we'll remove it during the grinding phase. We'll evaluate the condition during your estimate and explain what needs to happen before we begin.
