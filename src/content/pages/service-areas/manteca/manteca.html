Can You Coat Concrete In Outdoor Areas Like Patios Or Sidewalks?

How Do You Prepare Concrete Before Applying A Coating?

We mechanically grind the concrete to clean the surface and open the pores for bonding. This removes old sealers, stains, or flaking paint. If cracks or divots are present, we repair those before coating. This prep stage is critical to the performance of the system, and we never skip it. Poor prep leads to early failure.

Can You Coat Over An Existing Epoxy Floor?

Sometimes. If the old floor is structurally sound and not peeling, we can abrade it and install a new system over the top. If it's chipping or failing, we'll remove the old layer and start fresh. Every situation is different, so we'll inspect the surface during the quote and explain your best options.

Are These Floors Easy To Maintain?

Very. Most epoxy and polyurea coatings just need regular sweeping and the occasional mop with a pH-neutral cleaner. They resist stains, dust, and moisture. In high-use settings, an occasional clear recoat can help extend the life and shine. Because the floor is sealed, you won't have to deal with porous concrete absorbing oil or grime.

Do You Handle Concrete Repairs Or Just Coatings?

We handle both. Cracks, spalling, joint damage, and other surface problems are addressed before any coating is applied. We also offer joint expansion repair using flexible fillers that protect your slab from further movement or damage. If you're not sure whether your concrete needs repair or full resurfacing, we can talk through the options during your quote.

Can You Add Anti-Slip Texture For Safety?

Yes. We can add anti-slip materials to any coating system, including garage floors, warehouse aisles, medical facilities, and exterior walkways. These additives improve traction without making the floor feel sharp or abrasive. They're especially helpful in areas that see spills, rain, or vehicle traffic.

How Long Do These Coatings Last?

With proper prep and care, our concrete coatings typically last 10 to 15 years or more. In commercial environments with constant use, a recoat may be needed after several years. Regular cleaning and proper use of the space go a long way toward keeping the coating in great shape.
