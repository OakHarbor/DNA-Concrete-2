Our Professional Concrete Coating Process

Surface Preparation

A great epoxy or polished concrete floor starts with a clean, even surface. We start by grinding down the concrete to remove old coatings and create a strong bond for the new application. Any cracks or surface damage are filled and repaired to prevent flaws from affecting the final result. The surface is then thoroughly cleaned to remove dust, moisture, and contaminants, ensuring the coating adheres properly and lasts for years. If you skip these steps, you may see peeling, bubbling, or uneven finishes.

Application and Custom Options

Once the surface is ready, we apply multiple layers of epoxy or sealant, allowing for complete adhesion. We also offer custom finishes, anti-slip additives, and decorative patterns for both commercial and residential projects.

For polished concrete or staining, our team carefully applies even layers for a consistent look and finishes the surface with a protective sealant.

Can Old Coatings Be Removed?

Yes. We remove old or damaged coatings before applying a new epoxy layer. If an existing coating is failing---whether it's peeling, flaking, or losing durability---our grinding process strips it down to a smooth, fresh surface before a new application.

How Long It Takes To Apply and Cure

Most epoxy coatings take one to two days to install, depending on the size of the space. The curing process usually takes 24 to 72 hours, but fast-curing options are available for commercial spaces that need a quicker turnaround.

Can You Repair Cracks Before Coating Or Polishing?

Yes. Surface repair is part of every job we take on. We fix cracks, divots, and joint damage before applying coatings, stain, or polish. Repairing these flaws not only improves the final appearance, but also strengthens the surface and helps the finish last longer. For joints, we remove failing filler and replace it with a semi-rigid product designed to handle movement and weight.

What Is Joint Expansion Repair, And Why Does It Matter?

Concrete expands and contracts throughout the year. Expansion joints give the slab room to move without cracking. Over time, the filler inside these joints can break down or fall out. When that happens, the edges of the joint start chipping or separating. We clean the joint, refill it with the proper material, and shave it flush so the surface is even and protected. This repair is especially important in warehouses, garages, and high-traffic areas.

What's The Difference Between Polished Concrete And Epoxy?

Polished concrete is achieved by grinding and refining the surface to a smooth, reflective finish. It doesn't use coatings, but it's sealed to protect against stains and wear. Epoxy coatings involve applying resin-based layers over the concrete to create a sealed, durable surface. Both options are tough and low-maintenance, but they offer different looks and performance features. We can help you decide which is right for your space.

If you're not sure what's on your surface now, we can test it during the inspection and recommend the best removal and prep method.
