The Epoxy Application Process

Cleaning and Repairs Needed First?

The success of an epoxy floor depends on the condition of the existing concrete. Before application, we inspect the surface for cracks, uneven areas, or contamination that could affect adhesion. If needed, we perform joint expansion repairs, patch problem areas, and thoroughly clean the surface to prepare it for coating.

Adding the Epoxy to Your Floor

Once the surface is prepared, we apply a high-performance epoxy mix designed for long-term durability. The coating bonds seamlessly with the concrete, creating a non-porous, stain-resistant surface. Customization options include solid colors, metallic finishes, or decorative flake systems for added texture and visual appeal.
