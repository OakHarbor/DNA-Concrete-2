Stockton Pool Deck Resurfacing and Repair

A worn pool deck can create serious problems, and rough surfaces, cracks, and fading concrete make the space less enjoyable and harder to maintain. At DNA Restoring Concrete, we resurface and repair pool decks using high-performance epoxy systems that last for many years. Whether your deck feels slick when wet or just looks outdated, we install finishes that feel better underfoot and last much longer than paint or rubber coatings.

Epoxy Pool Decks That Last

Our epoxy coatings bond directly to concrete and create a surface that resists wear, water, and sun exposure. Once applied, the coating doesn't peel or flake, even under regular foot traffic. You get a clean finish that stays solid for years without the need for constant upkeep. Epoxy systems also give you a lot of flexibility in terms of color and texture. We can match the surrounding tile, patio, or landscape so that the deck blends in naturally.

Slip-Resistant and Comfortable to Walk On

We include traction additives in every deck we install. This extra grip helps prevent slips when the surface is wet, but doesn't make the texture rough or sharp. It still feels smooth under bare feet, even on the hottest days. These coatings also help the deck shed water better, which keeps the area drier and more stable after swimming.

Quick Project Turnarounds

Most resurfacing jobs wrap up within two to four days. That includes surface repair, grinding, coating, and topcoat application. We show up prepared and stay focused so you're not left wondering when the job will be done. Once finished, the surface cures quickly and is ready for light foot traffic not long after. If you're working around move-in dates or vacations, we can adjust the timing to fit.

Color Choices and Design Options

Epoxy comes in more than just solid colors. We offer flake blends that mimic natural stone, textured quartz for added grit, and metallic coatings with added dimension. You can go with something simple and neutral or try a bold, modern look. Before we begin, we'll show you sample boards and talk through different finishes that work well with your pool area. The goal is to create something that fits your space without adding maintenance headaches.

Repairing the Surface Before Coating

We don't start with coating---we start with repair. That includes cracks, surface peeling, and small holes where the concrete has broken down. We use outdoor-grade repair materials made to bond with concrete and withstand sun, water, and temperature swings. The repaired areas blend in with the rest of the deck once coated, and the finish holds up better when the base is solid.

Grinding and Prepping the Concrete

Before anything can bond properly, the surface needs to be prepared. We grind the concrete using commercial-grade equipment that opens up the pores and removes any paint, sealer, or old coating. After that, we clean and vacuum the area so dust doesn't interfere with adhesion. This prep work takes time, but it makes a big difference in how long the coating lasts. The bond is stronger, and the finish looks smoother and more even.

How Long Will a New Coating Last?

With the right prep and quality materials, most epoxy systems last ten to fifteen years. Lighter residential use tends to stretch that number out. For commercial or high-traffic properties, we sometimes recommend a clear topcoat every few years to extend the life of the original surface. Regular cleaning helps too, especially around the waterline where buildup can collect.

Working Over Painted or Previously Coated Surfaces

It's possible to coat over paint or an older deck finish, but only if the surface is still bonded and free from peeling. If it's flaking or worn thin, we remove it during the grinding process. During your estimate, we'll check the current condition and explain what prep work is needed before coating can begin. Some decks just need light prep. Others may require full removal of whatever was there before.

Best Season to Schedule Pool Deck Work

We schedule work year-round as long as the weather cooperates. Spring and early summer are popular times, since the temperatures are good for curing and most families haven't started using the pool yet. If you want the deck finished before a move-in, a backyard gathering, or another scheduled event, it helps to book ahead. We'll talk through the timing so it lines up with your plans.
