Our Concrete Floor Repair Process

1. Inspection & Damage Assessment

Every repair begins with a thorough evaluation of cracks, spalling, and underlying causes such as moisture intrusion or slab movement. This diagnosis guides the right repair method so the fix addresses the root problem, not just the surface symptom.

2. Surface Preparation & Demolition

Damaged and unsound concrete is removed using diamond grinding and saw-cutting equipment, with HEPA-filtered vacuum systems keeping dust disruption to your environment to a minimum while we expose a clean, stable substrate.

3. Structural Patch & Repair

We apply high-strength repair mortars or epoxy patching compounds engineered to bond permanently with existing concrete, restoring load-bearing strength and filling cracks and spalls flush with the surrounding slab.

4. Surface Leveling & Finishing

Repaired areas are ground and leveled to blend seamlessly with the surrounding floor, leaving a smooth, stable surface ready for sealing, coating, or your next flooring finish of choice.
