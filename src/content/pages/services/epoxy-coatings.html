Our Epoxy Concrete Service Process

1. Surface Preparation & Conditioning

Every successful epoxy application begins with meticulous preparation. Our diamond grinding equipment thoroughly cleans and conditions your concrete surface while our HEPA-filtered vacuum systems ensure minimal dust disruption to your environment.

2. Base Coat Application

We apply a high-grade wicking epoxy primer that penetrates deeply into the concrete substrate, filling minor imperfections and creating an unbreakable bond that prevents future delamination and prolongs the life of your floor.

3. Color & Design Implementation

Your pre-selected colors, patterns, and textures are carefully applied for a striking visual transformation. From solid colors to decorative flake systems and metallic finishes, we create exactly the look you've envisioned for your space.

4. Protective Clear Topcoat

A final clear topcoat seals and protects your new epoxy surface, enhancing stain resistance, improving cleanability, and providing long-lasting protection against UV fading and surface scratching.
