Our Concrete Overlay Process

1. Surface Preparation & Conditioning

Every successful overlay begins with meticulous preparation. Our diamond grinding equipment thoroughly cleans, profiles, and conditions your existing concrete surface while our HEPA-filtered vacuum systems ensure minimal dust disruption to your environment.

2. Crack & Repair Treatment

Before any overlay goes down, we address existing cracks, spalling, and surface damage so the new layer bonds to a stable, structurally sound base and won't telegraph old imperfections through the finished surface.

3. Overlay Application

We apply a polymer-modified overlay system in your chosen thickness and finish, whether a smooth microtopping, a stamped texture, or a decorative broadcast finish, carefully worked to achieve the exact look you've envisioned for your space.

4. Protective Sealing

A final sealer locks in color and texture while protecting your new overlay from moisture, staining, and everyday wear, extending its life and keeping it looking freshly finished for years to come.
