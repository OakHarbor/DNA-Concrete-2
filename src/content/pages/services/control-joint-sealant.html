Our Control Joint Sealant Process

1. Joint Inspection & Preparation

Every successful sealant application begins with a thorough inspection of existing joints for spalling, debris, or old failing sealant. Our diamond blade saws and HEPA-filtered vacuum systems clean and widen joints as needed while minimizing dust disruption to your environment.

2. Backer Rod Installation

For joints requiring additional depth control, we install closed-cell backer rod to establish the correct sealant depth-to-width ratio, ensuring proper flexibility and preventing three-sided adhesion that can cause premature failure.

3. Sealant Application

We apply a semi-rigid epoxy or flexible polyurethane/polyurea sealant matched to your floor's traffic and movement needs, filling each joint completely and tooling it to a clean, level finish flush with the surrounding slab.

4. Cure & Final Inspection

Once cured, every joint is inspected for full adhesion and a smooth, trip-free profile, giving you a protected floor ready for immediate traffic and long-term performance.
