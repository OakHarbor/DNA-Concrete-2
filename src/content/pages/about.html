About DNA

Andrew Billings started DNA after 15 years grinding, polishing, and coating concrete for other companies. He started it as a single dad, which shaped how he runs it: show up when you say you will, and do the work right the first time.

In 2023, Justin Lewis joined to run the office side: estimating, scheduling, and keeping clients in the loop. Justin came from restaurants, where you learn fast that people remember how you treated them as much as the food. That's how we run every job. You'll know what's happening, when, and why.

We're a small, family-owned team based in Lodi. We work with homeowners, business owners, and general contractors across the Central Valley and Sacramento area.
