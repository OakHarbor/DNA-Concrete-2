About Us

Our Legacy of Concrete Transformation

Founded in 2019 by concrete specialist Andrew Billings, DNA Restoring Concrete emerged from a perfect blend of technical mastery and personal determination. Drawing on over 15 years of hands-on concrete refinishing experience, Andrew established the company while balancing single fatherhood, driven by his vision to revolutionize how businesses and homeowners experience concrete surfaces. His expertise transforms utilitarian concrete into sophisticated, high-performance spaces that enhance property aesthetics and functionality.

DNA Restoring Concrete reached a pivotal growth milestone in 2023 with the addition of Justin Lewis, whose distinguished hospitality background brought a new dimension to our client relationships. Justin's customer-focused approach ensures that each project delivers both technical excellence and an exceptional client journey. As certified members of the American Society of Concrete Contractors, we deliver premium polished concrete, epoxy solutions, and specialized finishes throughout Stockton and Central Valley—creating durable, visually striking surfaces for commercial facilities, corporate environments, and residential properties alike.
