Commercial Concrete Coating Services We Offer

Commercial Epoxy Floor Coatings

Epoxy is still one of the most popular flooring solutions for commercial properties because it delivers great durability without excessive maintenance. A properly installed epoxy system creates a seamless surface that stands up to traffic, equipment, spills, and daily wear.

Many warehouses, production facilities, dealerships, and retail businesses choose epoxy because it is easy to clean and provides a professional appearance. Depending on the environment, we can also incorporate textures that improve traction and safety.

Concrete Floor Overlays and Micro-Toppings

In many cases, a concrete overlay provides an opportunity to transform a worn surface into something that looks completely different.

Flooring overlays, decorative overlays, and micro-toppings can refresh aging concrete while preserving the existing slab beneath. Once installed, the overlay can be polished, stained, sealed, or finished in a variety of ways. This approach often makes sense for commercial properties that want a new appearance without the disruption and expense of complete removal and replacement.

Commercial Concrete Floor Repair

Before any coating or finish is applied, the concrete itself must be in good condition. Cracks, spalling, impact damage, and chemical deterioration can all affect the performance of a finished floor.

We repair damaged concrete surfaces so they provide a solid foundation for coatings, polishing, or staining. In many facilities, repairing the floor early prevents larger issues from developing later.

Joint Expansion Repair

Joint failure is one of the most common problems we see in commercial buildings. Forklift traffic, rolling loads, and years of movement can break down joint material and damage slab edges.

We remove failed materials, repair damaged areas, and install products designed for commercial and industrial environments. A properly maintained joint system helps protect the surrounding slab and supports smoother facility operations.

Concrete Polishing

Many businesses prefer polished concrete because it provides durability without adding a coating layer. The polishing process hardens and refines the surface, reducing dust while creating a cleaner appearance.

Polished concrete works particularly well in warehouses, offices, showrooms, and large retail environments. Many property owners also appreciate the reduced maintenance requirements compared to other flooring options.

Concrete Staining and Decorative Finishes

Concrete staining allows property owners to add character and color while maintaining the natural look of concrete. We offer both acid-based and water-based stain systems depending on the appearance clients want to achieve.

Restaurants, showrooms, retail stores, and customer-facing spaces often benefit from decorative finishes that help create a more inviting environment.

Concrete Sealing

Concrete sealing provides an added layer of protection against moisture, staining, and daily wear. Some facilities use sealing as a standalone solution, while others incorporate it as part of a larger restoration project.

Sealers can be applied to interior and exterior commercial surfaces and help extend the lifespan of the concrete beneath.
